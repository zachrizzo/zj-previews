About

A Lo Vegano is a fully vegan Cuban kitchen inside Market on South in the Milk District, from the founders of Vegan Scoops. The menu runs from all-day brunch (waffles, guava swirl pancakes, croissant breakfast sandwiches) to Cuban plates like plant-based ropa vieja, mojo pork bowls and picadillo empanadas, with flan and pastelitos for dessert.
