About

The Red Scarlet Salon is an inclusive, full-service salon at 717 E Washington St in Thornton Park, downtown Orlando. The team specializes in dimensional color and blonding, precision cuts and hair extensions, and also offers Russian nail services. Walk-ins are welcome, parking in the salon's lot is free, and stylists speak English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Russian and Arabic.
