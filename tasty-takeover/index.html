About

Tasty Takeover (formerly Tasty Tuesdays) brings a rotating lineup of up to 12 food trucks and pop-ups to the back parking lots of 2424 E Robinson St every Tuesday from 6:30 to 10pm. Pick dinner from a few different trucks, then take it inside the neighboring bars to eat with a drink. Leashed pets are welcome, and free parking is a block east at Festival Park.
