About

Red Panda Noodle is a noodle food truck parked at À La Cart on N Orange Ave in downtown Orlando. The menu centers on wavy udon, tossed in bourbon sauce or with velvet beef and broccoli, plus chicken and pork chunks to snack on. Order ahead for pickup on the truck's Square page.
