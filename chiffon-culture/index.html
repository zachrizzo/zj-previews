About

Chiffon Culture is a bakery cafe that specializes in cakes, pastries, coffee and boba, with shops on Corrine Drive in Audubon Park and on Red Bug Lake Road in Winter Springs. The case rotates through croissants, Korean-style buns, egg tarts, Basque cheesecake and mousse cakes, alongside milk teas and fruit teas. The Corrine Drive shop identifies as women-owned and Asian-owned.
