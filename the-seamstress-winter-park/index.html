About

The Seamstress has been a Winter Park shop since it was founded in 1982. Owner Nancy Pham brings more than 30 years of experience, including time at Macy's, Nordstrom and Bloomingdale's and in her mother's bridal shop. The shop specializes in bridal and formalwear, men's tailoring, custom clothing and alterations. There's a private parking lot for customers.
